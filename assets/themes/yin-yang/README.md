# Yin Yang

Place Yin Yang biome and encounter artwork in this folder.

Suggested filenames:

- `yin-yang-room.png` - normal room artwork
- `yin-yang-arena.png` - boss arena artwork
- `yin-yang-minion.png` - standard creature
- `yin-yang-tank.png` - heavy creature
- `yin-yang-boss.png` - boss resting pose
- `yin-yang-boss-attack.png` - boss attack pose
- `yin-yang-attack.png` - separate attack effect
- `yin-yang-boss-sword-ready.png` - sword-equipped resting pose
- `yin-yang-boss-sword-overhead.png` - raised overhead chop pose
- `yin-yang-boss-sword-roundhouse.png` - horizontal roundhouse pose
- `yin-yang-boss-sword-slam.png` - arena-wide ground slam pose
- `yin-yang-overhead-1.png` through `yin-yang-overhead-3.png` - complete overhead swing frames
- `yin-yang-roundhouse-1.png` through `yin-yang-roundhouse-3.png` - complete roundhouse swing frames
- `yin-yang-ground-slam-1.png` through `yin-yang-ground-slam-3.png` - complete ground-slam frames

Keep gameplay sprites on transparent backgrounds. Room and arena artwork should fill the full canvas.
